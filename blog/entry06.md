## Content 
Giving my presentation in class and sharing my Student Success App at the Expo helped me improve my speaking skills and confidence. I learned that it is important to explain the problem first so people understand why the app is helpful. My app helps students stay organized, keep track of assignments, and reach their goals.
I also learned that I need to keep my explanations simple and clear. 

In class, I had more time to talk about the app, but during the Expo elevator pitch, I had to explain it quickly. This taught me how to share the most important information in a short amount of time.

Another thing I learned was that practicing before presenting makes a big difference. Practice helped me feel more confident and answer questions more easily. Showing visuals and examples of the app also helped people understand it better.

Overall, both presentations helped me become a better speaker and taught me how to explain my ideas clearly to different audiences.
